Showcase KCS

Jailbreak'li iPad veya iPhone'unu kablosuz CarPlay ekranına dönüştür.

Hotspot şart değil. Ayarlar → Wi-Fi Kurulumu → Mevcut Wi-Fi ağı yolunu seçip iki cihazı aynı taşınabilir modeme bağlayabilirsin.

Bu sürüm Amine Rostane'nin özgün Showcase projesi temel alınarak Kerem Cloud Services tarafından geliştiriliyor. Kaynak kodu GitHub'da; lisans GPL-3.0-or-later.

Compatibility

  • iOS 12 or newer.
  • KCS deposunda şu an iphoneos-arm rootful paket yayımlanıyor.
  • Rootless ve Taurine sürümleri için kaynak kodu mevcut, ancak KCS paketi henüz yayımlanmadı.
  • Requires Personal Hotspot or the receiver and iPhone connected to the same Wi-Fi router.

Notes

Showcase takes over classic Bluetooth while active and restores Apple's bluetoothd when stopped. Diagnostics and packet dumps are off by default. Enable them in Settings, under Troubleshooting, only when collecting a report.

Showcase needs internet to prepare CarPlay authentication. It stays ready across launches for up to 12 hours.

Tap with three fingers during CarPlay to reveal the stop and settings controls. Tap anywhere to return to CarPlay.

Anonim kullanım paylaşımı KCS sürümünde varsayılan olarak kapalıdır. Ayarlar → Anonim Kullanım'dan açıp gönderilen alanları seçebilirsin. Tanılama günlükleri yalnız sen onaylarsan KCS'ye yüklenir; arşiv Wi-Fi adları ve Bluetooth kayıtları içerebilir. Sunucu kodu açıktır.

  • Adds shared-network mode for portable Wi-Fi routers; Personal Hotspot can remain off when the iPad and iPhone use the same network.

  • Adds a star button on the home screen that links to the GitHub repository.
  • Adds anonymous usage reporting, on by default. Settings > Anonymous Usage turns it off or turns off single items.

1.0 Beta 5

  • Settings > Display has a resolution picker that follows your screen's shape, like on a Mac, and a 30 or 60 fps choice.
  • Prioritize Quality streams the largest resolution at 60 fps. Prioritize Performance replaces Prefer Smooth Video and keeps your setting.
  • Showcase now speaks French, Spanish, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. It follows your device's language; Settings > Language picks another.
  • Taurine users on iOS 14: install Showcase for Taurine instead. It replaces this package.

1.0 Beta 4-3

  • Fixes the false hotspot name warning on iOS 16 and newer.

1.0 Beta 4-2

  • Reconnects to your iPhone by itself, like a car. Open Showcase and CarPlay comes back without touching the iPhone.
  • Starts CarPlay when you open Showcase. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Stops cleanly and waits for your iPhone when it leaves or turns off Wi-Fi, instead of freezing on the last frame.
  • Rootless: keeps the Bluetooth pairing after a reboot, so you no longer need to forget the car on the iPhone.
  • Relaunching no longer asks for internet, Try Again works, and Showcase retries when the network comes back.
  • Opens without the long black screen.
  • Warns when the hotspot name you entered doesn't match the device name.

1.0 Beta 4

  • Starts Personal Hotspot by itself. If iOS refuses, Showcase asks you to turn it on in Settings.
  • Removes the 5 GHz hotspot option. The hotspot uses the band iOS chooses.
  • Choose where sound plays by tapping this device or the CarPlay sender on the Sound page.
  • Cleaner screens with shorter connection steps and no small text.
  • Tap anywhere on the dimmed screen to return to CarPlay.

1.0 beta 3-2

  • Starts Personal Hotspot by itself when you tap Start CarPlay. No trip to Settings.
  • On older devices, runs the CarPlay hotspot on 5 GHz instead of crowded 2.4 GHz, with automatic fallback.
  • Leaves other Wi-Fi networks during CarPlay so the radio stays on the hotspot.
  • Fixes Bluetooth using about a full CPU core after a session on rootful devices.
  • Adds a Settings screen behind the gear button with Cars, Hotspot, Sound, Display, Troubleshooting, and About.
  • Sound plays from your iPhone by default again. Turn on Play Sound on This iPad in Settings to route CarPlay audio to the receiver.
  • Shows each connection step while your iPhone connects.
  • Sizes the screen stream's TCP window like Apple's receiver, so older Wi-Fi chips stop discarding video bursts.
  • Sends touch reports at voice priority on the hotspot link.
  • Adds Dock on the Right, Prefer Smooth Video, and Start CarPlay Automatically.

  • Adds Apple ConvergedIPC/Skywalk HCI and ACL support.

  • Routes navigation, call, radio, and media audio through the receiver.
  • Selects an HD stream from native aspect ratio and measured hardware capability.
  • Schedules H.264 frames on the sender timeline and drops stale work after a stall.
  • Keeps touch reports responsive during motion.
  • Restores paired sessions after reconnects and app lifecycle changes.

  • Reports unsupported Bluetooth hardware instead of stalling during setup.
